Who We Are

More Than a Software Company

NexaTech Solutions is a global enterprise software company headquartered in Cape Town, South Africa. We design, build, and support a comprehensive suite of business software products — from cloud infrastructure to cybersecurity, data analytics, and process automation.

With offices across Africa, Europe, and Asia, and a distributed team of 200+ engineers, designers, and support specialists, we serve 500+ enterprise clients in 30+ countries. Every product we ship is built with a singular focus: making your business more resilient, efficient, and competitive.
